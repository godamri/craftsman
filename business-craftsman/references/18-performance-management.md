# Outcome-Based Performance Management & Anti-KPI Gaming

> **Core Principle**: When a measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a good measure (Goodhart's Law). Measure teams on business outcomes and system reliability, never on superficial activity metrics.

---

## 1. Toxic Metrics vs True Outcome Metrics

| Toxic Vanity Metrics (Prohibited) | True Outcome & Reliability Metrics (Mandated) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lines of Code Written** | Net Customer Retention & Revenue Generated |
| **Number of Tickets Closed** | Lead Time & Cycle Time for Verified Features |
| **Story Points / Velocity Velocity Theater** | Change Failure Rate (CFR) & MTTR |
| **Hours Worked / Online Status** | Customer Problem Resolution Rate |
| **Number of Meetings Attended** | System Availability & SLO Conformance |

---

## 2. The Team Health Balanced Scorecard

Evaluate team performance across 4 balanced pillars:
1. **Value Delivery**: Did the team ship capabilities that moved approved business metrics?
2. **Operational Quality**: Did the team maintain low defect rates, high uptime, and zero P0 security regressions?
3. **Delivery Flow**: Are cycle times predictable with low WIP and low dependency wait times?
4. **Learning & Adaptability**: Did the team rapidly falsify bad hypotheses and adopt systemic improvements?
