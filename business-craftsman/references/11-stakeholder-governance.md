# Stakeholder Governance & Single Outcome Ownership

> **Core Principle**: When everyone owns an initiative, nobody owns it. Every major initiative must have exactly one named Accountable Outcome Owner.

---

## 1. The Single Outcome Owner (DACI / RACI)

Every initiative must establish an unambiguous decision rights matrix:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ DRIVER (Accountable)    : The single named individual driving execution and outcome.    │
│ APPROVER (Decision)     : The executive with budget authority to greenlight/kill.      │
│ CONTRIBUTORS (Consulted): Subject matter experts contributing work or feedback.        │
│ INFORMED (Notified)     : Stakeholders updated on progress and major milestone shifts. │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Accountabilities by Role:
- **Executive Sponsor**: Protects budget, removes political blockers, enforces strategic alignment.
- **Product Manager**: Owns the business case, customer problem definition, and value realization.
- **Engineering Lead**: Owns technical correctness, architecture, reliability, and security execution.
- **Project Manager**: Owns schedule forecasting, critical path dependencies, risk tracking, and change control.
- **Operational Lead**: Owns production operability, runbooks, and ongoing supportability.
