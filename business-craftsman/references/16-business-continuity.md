# Business Continuity & Multi-Dimension Crisis Recovery

> **Core Principle**: Business continuity is broader than IT disaster recovery. Restoring servers does not mean the business has recovered.

---

## 1. The 5 Dimensions of Enterprise Recovery

```text
1. TECHNICAL RECOVERY   : Systems, databases, networks, and applications restored to operational state.
2. BUSINESS RECOVERY    : Core revenue operations, order fulfillment, and payroll re-established.
3. CUSTOMER RECOVERY    : Customers proactively notified, affected accounts credited, lost transactions replayed.
4. FINANCIAL RECOVERY   : Insurance claims processed, liquidity stabilized, delayed revenue collected.
5. REPUTATIONAL RECOVERY: Transparent executive communication, regulatory disclosures filed, customer trust restored.
```

---

## 2. Mitigation of Key-Person & Vendor Concentration Risk

- **Key-Person Risk**: No single engineer or operator may hold sole access or undocumented knowledge over critical systems. Enforce shared operational runbooks and access rotation.
- **Multi-Vendor Redundancy**: For critical business paths (e.g. payment processing, SMS verification), maintain a secondary pre-integrated fallback vendor.
