# Project Management & Delivery Taxonomy

> **Core Principle**: Conflating Projects, Products, and Operations leads directly to governance chaos. Structure execution around explicit organizational entities.

---

## 1. The Organizational Entity Taxonomy

| Entity | Definition | Primary Objective | Success Criterion | Lifecycle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PORTFOLIO** | High-level collection of strategic investments. | Maximize ROIC and strategic position. | Net Value Realized & Risk Contained. | Permanent / Ongoing. |
| **INITIATIVE** | Broad strategic effort spanning multiple projects/teams. | Deliver a major business capability. | Strategic Goal Achieved. | Finite (6–18 months). |
| **PRODUCT** | Long-lived asset delivering ongoing customer value. | Maximize customer LTV, adoption, and margin. | Retention, Revenue, Customer Satisfaction. | Multi-year / Continuous evolution. |
| **PROJECT** | Temporary endeavor to create a specific output. | Deliver defined scope within budget and schedule. | Quality output delivered to specification. | Temporary (Weeks/Months). |
| **PROGRAM** | Group of related projects managed cooperatively. | Coordinate shared dependencies and benefits. | Synergistic Program Benefits Realized. | Finite (1–3 years). |
| **OPERATION** | Ongoing, recurring business activity. | Maximize efficiency, reliability, and uptime. | SLA, Unit Cost, Operational SLOs. | Permanent / Continuous. |

---

## 2. The Project Charter Core

Every approved project must have a signed **Project Charter** containing:
1. **Business Case Link**: Approved investment budget and target outcome metric.
2. **Defined Scope & Explicit Non-Goals**: Bound deliverables.
3. **Critical Path & Milestones**: Major sequencing with hard dependency constraints.
4. **RACI Matrix**: Single named Project Manager, Product Owner, and Engineering Lead.
5. **Pre-Planned Stop/Kill Criteria**: Failure thresholds that trigger mandatory project review.
