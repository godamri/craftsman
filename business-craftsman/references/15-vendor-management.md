# Procurement, Vendor Governance & Buy vs Build

> **Core Principle**: Build your core competitive differentiator; buy or consume standard commodities. Every critical vendor contract must have a credible, pre-tested exit strategy.

---

## 1. The Buy vs Build Decision Matrix

```text
                               HIGH STRATEGIC DIFFERENTIATION
                                             ▲
                                             │
                        BUILD IN-HOUSE       │    PARTNER / CO-DEVELOP
                  (Core IP, Competitive Moat)│ (High complexity, shared advantage)
                                             │
      ───────────────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────────────►
                                             │                                      COMMODITY
                        OPEN SOURCE          │         BUY COMMERCIAL SAAS
                  (Standard infrastructure)  │    (Non-differentiating utility:
                                             │     Payroll, Email, CRM, Helpdesk)
                                             ▼
                                LOW STRATEGIC DIFFERENTIATION
```

---

## 2. Vendor Governance Invariants

1. **Data Ownership & Portability**: Contracts must explicitly grant full customer data ownership and mandate automated daily data exports in standard open formats.
2. **Documented Exit Strategy**: Before signing a contract for a critical vendor, document how the organization would migrate away if the vendor triples prices, gets acquired, or suffers persistent outages.
3. **SLA & Financial Remedies**: Enforce binding SLAs with direct financial credits and termination rights for persistent SLA breaches.
