# Product Management & Problem Discovery

> **Core Principle**: Fall in love with the customer's problem, not your internal solution. Building features without verified adoption and retention is waste.

---

## 1. The Problem Validation Formula

A customer problem is worthy of investment only if it satisfies:

$$\text{Problem Viability} = \text{High Frequency} \times \text{High Severity} \times \text{High Willingness to Pay / Adopt}$$

```text
PROBLEM DISCOVERY CHECKLIST:
[ ] Who specifically experiences this problem (job role, persona, company stage)?
[ ] How do they currently solve it (spreadsheets, competitors, manual workarounds)?
[ ] Why is the current workaround inadequate or painful?
[ ] What measurable metric will the customer use to judge our product's success?
[ ] Have at least 10 target customers confirmed this problem with concrete evidence?
```

---

## 2. The Anti-Feature-Factory Manifesto

1. **Customer Request $\neq$ Roadmap Commitment**: A customer requesting Feature $X$ is describing an attempted solution. Discover the underlying root problem before agreeing to build anything.
2. **True MVP Definition**: An MVP is the **smallest experiment that delivers real customer value or extracts maximum validated learning**, NOT a broken, buggy version of a giant product.
3. **Product Sunset Policy**: Products and features that fail to achieve threshold active adoption or generate negative unit economics must have a formal sunset/decommissioning schedule.
