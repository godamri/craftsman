# Stop-Loss Governance & Initiative Termination Criteria

> **Core Principle**: Stopping a value-destroying initiative is a victorious management decision that liberates scarce capital and talent. Failure lies in continuing an initiative after evidence proves it unviable.

---

## 1. Explicit Kill Criteria Thresholds

An initiative, project, or feature must be subjected to mandatory **Termination Review** if ANY of the following 6 thresholds are breached:

```text
1. ECONOMIC THRESHOLD     : Projected TCO exceeds revised Expected Value by > 25%, or Payback Period > 24 months.
2. ADOPTION THRESHOLD     : Post-launch active customer adoption is < 30% of target after 60 days.
3. STRATEGIC MISALIGNMENT : Market or company strategy shifts, rendering the initiative outside top-3 priorities.
4. DEPENDENCY FAILURE     : Critical third-party partner or technology proves unviable with zero feasible contingency.
5. SCHEDULE STOP-LOSS     : Initiative exceeds its P90 delivery forecast by > 50% without reaching Beta.
6. REGULATORY VETO        : Compliance or legal counsel identifies unmitigatable regulatory liability.
```

---

## 2. The 4-Step Graceful Kill Protocol

When an initiative is terminated:
1. **Document the Learning (Kill Memo)**: Record the exact empirical evidence and assumptions falsified.
2. **Safely Decommission & Archive**: Clean up temporary cloud infrastructure, revoke vendor licenses, and archive codebase.
3. **Reallocate Capital & Talent**: Immediately redeploy the team to the highest-priority backlog initiative.
4. **Celebrate Decision Discipline**: Publicly recognize the team for transparent reporting and swift stop-loss execution.
