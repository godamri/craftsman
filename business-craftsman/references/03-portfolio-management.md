# Portfolio Governance & Capital Allocation

> **Core Principle**: A project being "on track" does NOT mean it should continue. Portfolios must be dynamically evaluated based on updated expected value and opportunity cost.

---

## 1. The 11 Portfolio Lifecycle States

```text
PROPOSED ──> DISCOVERY ──> APPROVED ──> FUNDED ──> ACTIVE ──> COMPLETED ──> VALUE_REALIZED
   │            │             │           │          │
   └──(KILLED)──┴──(CANCELLED)┴───────────┴──(PAUSED/AT_RISK)
```

---

## 2. Dynamic Portfolio Re-Funding Test

At every monthly portfolio review, every active initiative must answer:

> **"Knowing what we know today about market feedback, technical complexity, cost, and competitors, would we fund this initiative from scratch today?"**

- **If YES**: Continue or accelerate funding.
- **If NO**: Immediately execute **Stop-Loss / Kill / Pivot Protocol**. Sunk cost is never an acceptable justification for continuation.

---

## 3. Portfolio Allocation Horizons (The 70 / 20 / 10 Rule)

- **Core (70% Capital/Capacity)**: Optimizing, defending, and growing existing proven business lines.
- **Adjacent (20% Capital/Capacity)**: Expanding into adjacent markets, customer segments, or related capabilities.
- **Transformational (10% Capital/Capacity)**: High-risk, high-reward strategic options and frontier bets.
